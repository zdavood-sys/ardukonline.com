ARDUK

A persistent medieval fantasy civilisation sandbox

ARDUK is an original online world in early development by Anar Studios: players gather, build and govern settlements in a world that persists between sessions.

The game is not available to the public. There is no sign-up, store or download on this site. Closed tests are by personal invitation only.
