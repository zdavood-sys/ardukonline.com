Privacy policy

ARDUK by Anar Studios · Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data ARDUK collects, why, and what you can do about it. ARDUK is in early development; the game is available only to people personally invited to closed tests.

Who is responsible

Anar Studios, the developer of ARDUK. Contact us through the ARDUK Discord.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts. It doesn't ask you for any information. The hosting provider may record technical data such as your IP address in its own server logs to deliver and protect the site; see its privacy statement (GitHub).

The game: signing in with Epic Games

Invited testers sign in with their Epic Games account through Epic Online Services. Epic handles the sign-in; we never see or store your Epic password. Epic's own privacy policy applies to Epic's processing: epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy.

When you sign in, we receive and keep:

DataWhy
Your Epic account ID and the game-specific user ID Epic issues for ARDUKTo recognise your account each time you sign in
Your Epic display nameShown to other players in the game's player list
An internal ARDUK account number, when the account was created and when you last signed inTo run your account
Account status (active or disabled), and if disabled: when, by whom and whyTo enforce test rules and keep a record of moderation decisions
The IP address you connect from, and the time and result of each sign-in attemptSecurity: limiting repeated failed sign-in attempts and investigating abuse

Sign-in tokens from Epic are checked once and not stored; only a one-way fingerprint is kept until the token expires, to stop it being reused.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent personal data, show advertising, or use your data for tracking or profiling. We don't collect payment details. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the game (Epic for sign-in; the provider hosting our game servers), or where the law requires it.

Where and how long

Account data is stored on servers operated by Anar Studios. It is kept while the closed tests run. It is deleted when you ask us to delete your account, or when a test world and its accounts are reset. Security logs are kept only as long as needed to protect the service.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account, by contacting us through the ARDUK Discord. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to processing or to complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

ARDUK's closed tests are not intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly invite them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
